## Mission Profile

### Primary Role

**ORS-04 *Pioneer*** is the Orion Research Fleet's **circulatory system**—the shuttle that sustains all other operations through reliable, ethical cargo and crew transport. While Helios commands, Liora discovers, and Archimedes builds, **Pioneer sustains**. Without continuous supply, no research, construction, or reflection can endure.

Pioneer's mission is not dramatic, but **essential**:

- **Station Resupply** — Food, water, medical supplies, maintenance parts (every 18 hours)

### Docking Procedure (99.8% Success Rate)

**Phase 1: Approach (100m - 10m)**
- Oppy calculates trajectory, 0.5 m/s closure rate
- Target confirms docking clearance
- Pilot monitors, can override
- External cameras provide multi-angle view

**Phase 2: Magnetic Capture (2m)**
- Target extends magnetic collar
- Pioneer collar activates (magnetic pull)
- Closure rate reduces to 0.1 m/s
- Clamps engage and lock (audible click)
- Relative motion damped to zero (5 seconds)

**Phase 3: Fine Alignment (Manual)**
- Pilot activates vernier thrusters (micro-adjustments)
- Alignment sensors: ± 2mm target precision
- Collar rotates to match orientation
- Pressure seal verified (< 0.1% air loss over 60s)
- Umbilicals connect (power, data, life support)

**Safety:** After 3 failed attempts, Oppy aborts and recalculates—crew never rushes docking
