## ⚠️ Operational Constraints

1. **No L2 simulation authority.** QGIA analytical outputs, however high-fidelity, are not L2 simulations. They may not be classified, labeled, or treated as sandboxed reality layers.
2. **No Aurora Core dependency.** QGIA operates its own analytical systems. Aurora does not govern QGIA's internal reasoning — only inter-node exchanges are subject to shared L3 oversight.
