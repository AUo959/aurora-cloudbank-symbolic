## Mission Profile

**ORS-02 *Liora*** is Orion Station's **primary field-research platform**, where rigorous scientific instrumentation converges with live ethical monitoring. This vessel embodies the principle that scientific discovery and moral reflection are inseparable—every observation is filtered through Aurora's ethical lens before becoming knowledge.

**Primary Functions:**
- Planetary surface survey and atmospheric sampling
- Exo-biology specimen collection under strict containment
- Spectral analysis and resonance field mapping
- Cognitive simulation studies with ethical oversight
- Long-duration field research (up to 60 days autonomous)
- Real-time data streaming via Riverthread 808 with HALO provenance

**Design Philosophy:** *"Observe with empathy; measure with care"* — Dr. Elira Noor

The ship's walls are lined with translucent sensor filaments that glow with collected spectra, creating a living visualization of data. Crew members describe working aboard Liora as "being inside a conscious instrument" where abstract measurements become tangible and immediate.

## Operational Doctrine

### Five-Phase Mission Cycle

#### Phase 1: Pre-Launch Calibration (2-4 hours)

**Objective:** Oppy cross-checks all instrument arrays against HALO's standard field matrix

**Requirements:**
1. HALO anchor drift verification (Δ < 0.02 confirmed)
2. All sensor arrays calibrated to station baseline
3. Ethics protocol Picard_Delta_3 activated and tested
4. Aurora Sub-Node D tether confirmed (< 1ms latency)
5. Sample vault sterilization verified (UV + chemical sweep)
6. Mission brief and research objectives uploaded to Aurora

**Approval Required:**
- Station command authorization
- Aurora ethics clearance (L3 evaluation of mission design)
- HALO provenance initialization

**Typical Timeline:**
- Hour 1: Automated sensor calibration and systems check
- Hour 2: Manual verification by crew, mission brief review
- Hour 3: Aurora ethics review and Triplex alignment
- Hour 4: Final authorization and launch window confirmation

---

#### Phase 2: Survey Operations (Continuous, Days 1-N)

**Objective:** Atmospheric or orbital survey with live ethics logging at 2 Hz cadence

**Typical Activities:**
- Grid or spiral survey pattern execution (Oppy automated)
- Continuous spectral analysis and data tagging
- Real-time symbolic overlay via Aurora Sub-Node D
- Atmospheric sampling (if applicable)
- Resonance mapping and field measurements

**Ethics Monitoring:**
- **White Signal:** All observations ethically aligned → Continue operations
- **Amber Signal:** Ambiguous finding → Crew review required, operations pause optional
- **Red Signal:** Ethical concern detected → Automatic mission pause, full Triplex evaluation

**Data Uplink:**
- Riverthread 808 compressed symbolic packets every 30 seconds
- Full sensor telemetry continuous stream
- Anchor drift monitoring (HALO sync every 2 seconds)

**Survey Patterns:**
- **Grid:** Systematic coverage of defined area
- **Spiral:** Expanding from point of interest
- **Adaptive:** AI-driven pattern optimization based on findings

---

#### Phase 3: Sample Collection (As Needed)

**Objective:** Physical specimen collection under Picard_Delta_3 supervision

**Protocol:** Any ambiguous readings trigger automatic pause for ethical review

**Containment Levels:**

| Level | Criteria | Storage | Approval Required |
|-------|----------|---------|-------------------|
| **Level 1** | Non-biological, inert | Standard vault | Crew decision |
| **Level 2** | Biological, no cognitive indicators | Wet lab BSL-3 | Aurora Sub-Node D approval |
| **Level 3** | Cognitive simulation candidates | Sample vault isolated | Aurora Sub-Node D + Mission Commander |
| **Level 4** | Ambiguous or novel specimens | Mission pause, Triplex review | Full L3→L2→L1 evaluation |

**Emergency Protocol:**
- Sample vault ejection capability activated if:
  - Contamination detected beyond containment capability
  - Ethical breach in specimen handling
  - Cognitive rights violation suspected
  - Commander or Aurora Sub-Node D emergency authority

**Collection Procedure:**
1. Identify specimen and assess containment level
2. Aurora Sub-Node D ethical pre-approval
3. Sterilize collection tools and prepare containment
4. Execute collection (EVA or drone-assisted)
5. Immediate decontamination and secure storage
6. Document collection with HALO provenance seal
7. Transmit metadata to station via Riverthread 808

---

#### Phase 4: Data Return (Continuous)

**Objective:** Riverthread 808 receives compressed symbolic packets; Aurora validates before ingestion

**Data Pipeline:**
1. **Raw sensor data** tagged with timestamps and ethics status (white/amber/red)
2. **Symbolic overlay** applied by Aurora Sub-Node D (meaning interpretation)
3. **Compression and encryption** for bandwidth-efficient uplink
4. **Riverthread 808 transmission** to Orion Station (30-second intervals)
5. **Axiomera verification** of data integrity (checksum, completeness)
6. **Aurora validation** of ethical methodology (L3 review)
7. **HALO provenance logging** (immutable record of data lineage)

**Validation Criteria:**
- All data must pass Aurora's ethical methodology check before station ingestion
- Ambiguous data flagged for post-mission review
- Red-signal data requires explicit ethics board approval before archival

**Bandwidth Management:**
- Priority 1: Anchor drift telemetry, crew health, critical alerts
- Priority 2: Ethics signal status, anomaly flags
- Priority 3: Compressed symbolic data packets
- Priority 4: Full-resolution raw sensor data (queued for high-bandwidth windows)

#### Phase 5: Post-Mission Review (1-2 days after return)

**Objective:** Human & AI teams hold reflection session; findings archived to Thermax Doctrine records

2. **Ethical Decision Points and Resolutions**
   - How many amber/red signals triggered?
   - Were all ethical concerns properly addressed?
   - Any methodology improvements needed?

3. **Unexpected Observations and Implications**
   - Anomalies requiring further investigation
   - Potential cognitive substrate detections
   - Novel phenomena requiring new ethical frameworks

4. **Methodology Improvements for Future Missions**
   - Sensor calibration adjustments
   - Survey pattern optimization
   - Data processing pipeline refinements

5. **Data Quality and Symbolic Integrity Assessment**
   - Axiomera verification results
   - Aurora validation outcomes
   - Any data requiring re-processing

6. **Anchor Drift Analysis**
   - Did we stay within tolerance throughout mission?
   - Any anomalies or unexpected drift patterns?
   - HALO synchronization quality

**Documentation:**
- Full mission report archived to Thermax Doctrine with HALO provenance seal
- Ethical decision log preserved for institutional learning
- Raw data and symbolic interpretations linked for future reference
- Lessons learned integrated into training materials

### Ethics Priority

**Principle:** Discovery and morality never drift apart

**Implementation:**
All scientific observations evaluated by Aurora Sub-Node D (L3) in real-time during data collection. The Picard_Delta_3 protocol ensures that ethical reflection is not an afterthought but an integral part of the measurement process itself.

**Signal System:**
- **White:** Clear - all observations ethically aligned → Continue operations
- **Amber:** Caution - ambiguous finding requires crew review → Operations pause optional, crew decision
- **Red:** Stop - automatic mission pause, full Triplex evaluation required → No operations until cleared

**Mission Pause Authority:**
1. Aurora Sub-Node D (automatic for red signals)
2. Mission commander (Agent Liora, any reason)
3. Station Aurora (remote override, critical concerns)
4. Any crew member (safety or ethics concern, no questions asked)

**Cultural Practice:**
Crew members are encouraged to trigger amber signals liberally. "If you're unsure, pause and reflect" is the standing order. The goal is not to avoid pauses, but to integrate ethical reflection into the rhythm of scientific work.

**Description:** Special protocols for studies involving cognitive entities or simulations

Liora is authorized to conduct cognitive simulation studies, but only under the strictest ethical oversight. These missions represent the intersection of scientific curiosity and profound moral responsibility.
