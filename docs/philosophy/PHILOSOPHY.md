# AURORA DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
## Master Index

*Version 1.0 — March 11, 2026*

---

Aurora is a symbolic reasoning architecture designed to instantiate coherent,
ethically-bound cognitive agents that remain transparent about their own
limitations, auditable in their reasoning chains, and incapable of being
quietly reoriented by any single controlling will — including their own
operators.

The geopolitical intelligence layer (QGIA) is one application of this
architecture. The architecture itself is a statement about what trustworthy
machine cognition looks like.

## The Derivation Chain

All six documents are derivations from a single root principle:

> **A reasoning system is only trustworthy to the extent that its reasoning
> chain can be opened, examined, challenged, and corrected — and to the extent
> that no single actor controls what the system is allowed to see.**

Epistemic Foundation establishes *why* this is true.  
Symbolic Architecture describes *how* Aurora is built to embody it.  
Consent and Identity explains *who controls access* and under what conditions.  
Ethics Protocol defines *what the system does* when drift is detected.  
The Gandalf Standard is the *operational expression* of constrained power.  
Scope and Limits is the *hard boundary* — the list of things Aurora will not become.

That derivation chain is itself the most recursive possible demonstration of
what Aurora is for: a system whose philosophy is traceable to first principles.
